Modality Cheat-Sheet (from my notes)

ModalityKey point
CT — arterial (25-30s)Poor tumour-vs-parenchyma contrast → easy to miss.
CT — pancreatic phase (40-45s)Detection phase. Normal pancreas enhances bright; PDAC stays dark (hypovascular) → best contrast.
CT — portal venous (60-70s)Staging: venous involvement & liver metastases.
MRI — T1 fat-satBest parenchymal sequence. Normal pancreas bright; PDAC dark (desmoplastic stroma).
MRI — T2PDAC mildly low/iso; T2 mainly for cysts/necrosis/mucin.
MRI — DWIPDAC restricts: DWI bright / ADC dark.
US (first line)Hypoechoic, ill-defined; limited by bowel gas (esp. tail). Doppler hypovascular.
EUSBest for <2 cm; tool of choice for FNA/FNB tissue.
