LI-RADS v2018 — LR-5 (Definitely HCC) Calculator

For at-risk patients only (cirrhosis / chronic HBV). LR-5 = observation ≥10 mm with nonrim arterial-phase hyperenhancement (APHE) plus the required number of additional major features.
